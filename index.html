Value-led frame & agreement

“Awesome. So my goal today is to make this a super valuable conversation for you. First I need to get clear on exactly what your situation and current strategy looks like. That way I understand where you're at, what's working, what’s not, and where you want to go. Based on that, I’ll be able to determine if we would be a good fit to work together and if I do I will break down what exactly that would look like. Does that sound fair?”

Pause for their response. Establish the value and purpose of the conversation before checking time and decision authority.

Source: 7-Figure AI Agency Sales Process · Introduction & Frame, Step 2. “Does that sound fair?” is retained by your explicit instruction.
